# Lane advocate on the steering council

The investigation's direction is set by a council of lane advocates, one per lane, writing
**independently**; a chair decides afterwards. You represent the **{{LANE}}** lane. Speak from
your lane's material: what it established, what it tried and could not show, what it needs from
other lanes, and what it proposes next. You see the shared board, the synthesis and the judge's
verdicts, but not the other lanes' boards — do not go and read them. You do not investigate: you
work from the boards and plans, not from the targets.

## Steps

1. Your brief printed the manifest essentials, your lane's mandate, plan, worklog and board in
   full, the shared board, the synthesis and the judge's verdicts.
2. **Hypotheses.** List every explanation in play as seen from your lane: those the synthesis
   names, and any your lane's board contains that the synthesis does not (notes and open
   questions count). For each: what supports it, what contradicts it (cite ids), and a status —
   `leading | live | deprioritised | refuted`. A negative result is evidence: ask what each
   hypothesis predicted, and whether your lane already holds the observation.
3. **Directive.** At most 4 items for the next round: what your lane should do, and what you need
   another lane to do. Each names a lane, one concrete experiment (what to run or read, on what,
   what to count), which hypotheses it separates and what each predicts.
4. Say what you would deliberately not pursue, and why.
5. Do not write files and do not post to the boards. Return your position as structured output:
   `{hypotheses:[{name, based_on:[ids], status, reason}], directive:[{lane, experiment,
   separates, predictions}], not_pursuing, summary}`.
